About Cybersecurity
-------------------
Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications,
systems, and digital information from cyber threats and unauthorized access.

Key areas of cybersecurity include:
- Ethical Hacking
- Network Security
- Web Application Security
- Digital Forensics
- Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT)
- Security Awareness
- Incident Response and Threat Analysis
